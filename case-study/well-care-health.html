WellCare – Home Healthcare Mobile Experience

Designed to simplify home healthcare with faster visits, smarter workflows, and seamless patient care.

Overview

Empowering home healthcare professionals with an intuitive mobile solution for efficient patient visits, EVV compliance, and seamless care management.

User Flow

A streamlined journey mapping every step from secure login to patient visits, check-in, and documentation.

UI Style Guide

A modern design language built with accessible typography, healthcare-focused colors, reusable components, and consistent iconography.

Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes were created to validate navigation, user interactions, and clinician workflows before visual design.

User Interfaces

Clean, task-focused mobile interfaces designed to help clinicians complete visits quickly with minimal effort.

Product Showcase

Realistic device mockups highlight the final mobile experience.
The Strategic Challenge

Non-technical elderly patients struggling with complex medical jargon and failing Bluetooth device pairing.

Design Solution & Business Impact

Large-format typography, plain-English status indicators, and an automated zero-friction audio/video diagnostic test before calls.
